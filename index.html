Proposed routes

Two seas, one week

Start from Athens and turn either way. Sheltered island-hopping in the Saronic Gulf, or the open blue of the Aegean and the Cyclades.

Saronic Gulf

Sheltered water, short hops and pine-covered islands an hour from Athens. Aegina, Poros, Hydra and the ancient theatre at Epidaurus.

Aegean & Cyclades

Whitewashed villages, thousands of bays and the deep blue of the open Aegean. Milos, Paros, Amorgos, Santorini.
