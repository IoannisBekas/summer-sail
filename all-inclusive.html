What it actually means

A sailing holiday means

  • Being carefree in the natural environment of the sea
  • Easy nights under the stars with your own company
  • Privacy — your own yacht and your own captain
  • Swimming off beaches you cannot reach by road
  • Dropping anchor beside remote tavernas with extraordinary food
  • Freedom — you draw your own line on the chart
  • Simplicity — no sunbeds, no hauling luggage; your things stay on board
  • Breakfast at the best table there is — your own deck, on the open blue
  • Safety, with only your own people around you
  • VIP but not a VIP price — real experiences at sensible rates
  • And many more experiences waiting to be discovered
